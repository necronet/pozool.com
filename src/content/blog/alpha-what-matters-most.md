Pozool Stays is in alpha. We are building a clearer way to manage properties, rentals, and all the work that happens between one reservation and the next.

![Pozool Stays dashboard](/dashboard-1.png)

The dashboard puts what is urgent first: arrivals, departures, occupancy, payments, ADR, RevPAR, and today’s reservations. It is a quick view to start the day knowing what needs attention.

![Pozool Stays availability calendar](/calendar-view.png)

The calendar shows availability across every listing on one timeline. That makes it easier to spot stays, gaps, and conflicts before they become a problem.

![Listing reservations in Pozool Stays](/listing-reservation-list.png)

Each listing has its own context: nightly price, cleaning fee, guests staying, and reservation history. Operations no longer need to be split across conversations, platforms, and spreadsheets.

![Manual reservation form in Pozool Stays](/reservation-create.png)

Manual and direct reservations can be created too. The flow brings the listing, guests, dates, prices, and payments together in one place, making every stay clear to record.

![Pozool Stays guest directory](/guest-list.png)

The guest directory keeps each person’s details and history together. This helps teams follow every stay without losing sight of the relationship with the person arriving at the property.

This alpha is our time to learn through real use, refine these flows, and make them useful before adding more complexity. We will soon create an onboarding process and open the tool to property owners in general. Stay tuned!

A quick note: some of the screenshots are currently in Spanish, but we hope the product is still easy to understand.
