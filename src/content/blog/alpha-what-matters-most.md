Pozool Stays is in alpha. What matters most is not an endless feature list: it is being able to manage properties, reservations, tasks, and expenses with less chaos and much more clarity.

![An apartment prepared to welcome guests](/airbnb-demo-1.jpg)

Every property has its own identity and details. That is why Stays starts with an organized foundation: properties, units, photos, location, and the information the team needs to find without searching through chats and spreadsheets.

![A short-term rental cabin with a sunset view](/airbnb-demo-2.png)

Short-term rentals move quickly: calendars, reservations, guests, payments, and availability. The alpha brings these workflows together so an operation can follow each listing from one place.

![A property with a pool ready for a stay](/airbnb-demo-3.jpg)

A reservation does not end once it is confirmed. Cleaning, maintenance, and expenses are also part of delivering a great experience. Stays connects them to properties and reservations so the operation does not lose context.

![An interior space full of personality](/airbnb-demo-4.jpg)

Not every property is managed the same way. Pozool Stays is built for mixed portfolios, where short stays coexist with longer rentals and each needs follow-up without creating separate processes.

![A living room ready to be lived in](/airbnb-demo-5.png)

This alpha stage is for learning through real use: refining what already exists, listening to early users, and making the tools genuinely useful before adding more complexity. The goal remains simple: give property managers a clearer view of their operation and more time to care for their properties and guests.

We will soon create an onboarding process and open the tool to property owners in general. Stay tuned!
